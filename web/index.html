Disconnected
  1. Disconnect the buds from your device's Bluetooth settings first; an old link blocks a new one.
  2. Take them out of the case.
  3. In the picker, choose the unnamed LE device (it has no name and a strong signal). Don't pick the "QCY ArcBuds — Paired" entry: that's Classic audio, and Chrome rejects it as "Unsupported device".
